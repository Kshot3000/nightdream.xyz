Midnight

NIGHT · live network · DUST model · bridge mental model

☾ NIGHT overview

Midnight is Cardano’s partner chain for shielded smart contracts. NIGHT is the native economic token; DUST is the renewable resource used to pay for shielded computation.

NIGHT redemption

The Glacier claim window closed Oct 20, 2025. Claimants' NIGHT unlocks in four 25% thaws, 90 days apart — we're in the final window now. Missed the claim? A ~252M NIGHT Lost-and-Found phase covers eligible users who didn't claim in time. NightDream can't see your claim — exact dates live in the official portal.
